Internal alignments & constraints -Architect Ironvale · Internal

Build vs buy alignment. This is a joint decision based on available information. I would give my POV (below), but would leave space that based on RFP discussions alone, we don't have enough data for a quality decision and need additional meetings/service to reliably make one.

Build vs buy -a layered approach

In the AI space, building and owning lives in the space of loops and layers. We propose owning what compounds and renting what commoditizes:

Own -Ironvale's proprietary claims data

Nearly a century of loss experience, closed files, and adjuster decisions across 750K policies and 14 states. We use this data to power evals, build guardrails, calibrate routing etc.

Build together, Ironvale owns -orchestration layer

Orchestration, routing logic, coverage heuristics, eval harness, decision logs. This is the place where Ironvale's adjuster/claims judgment gets encoded. This layer makes every other layer (model, vendor, orchestration pattern) swappable and is something we build together and Ironvale owns.

Rent -foundation models

Rented with model portability engineered in from day one, verified continuously by the eval harness so any provider can be swapped in days thus avoiding lock-in.

Decouple -core claims platform

We decouple during MVP and have read-only access through an adapter layer or read replica to avoid being blocked by migration timeline. When finished this layer gets swapped with APIs. This approach comes with a caveat that we don't write anything in the claims db for MVP. The MVP would craft recommendation and the adjuster would execute. Our voice and tone here is pragmatic, disciplined and transparent focusing on enabling clean de-coupling and then swapping.

Narrative for CTO: "You own what compounds. You rent what commoditizes." Our job is to help you accelerate your static dataset into a compounding asset -through an orchestration layer on top, so that everything below stays swappable.

MVP definition

An undefined MVP is an opportunity to set a realistic course.

Based on available data, and current system state we would jointly define first three slices. MVP = a thin lifecycle slice — one peril, one state. If we decide we don't have enough data to stand behind our decisions we would either make a calculated risk or execute a mitigation step I previously noted. In terms of the rest of value areas to cover, we would use strategic intent = An opinionated statement of the outcome we're steering toward which is more stable than roadmap items and shifting models, slices and features.

What I take into consideration when forming an MVP?

  • Time-to-value (speed of delivery)
  • High impact (solving an actual pain) - low friction entry points in terms of adoption with early adopters
  • Data state and low redtape conditions
  • Ability to clearly track impact
  • Mistakes are recoverable, visible, or cheap (low blast radius)
Slice example (once we know baseline)

Residential water-damage FNOL triage (Ohio, <$10K claims)

KPI: Cycle time to first-payment decision - Ohio residential water cohort

Hypothesis: AI intake at FNOL (photos + policy + loss description → draft estimate with confidence score) routes ~30% of this cohort to accelerated path, cutting cycle from ~11 days to <2 days

Build: Intake agent triggered on residential + Ohio + water + <$10K; drafts assessment + estimate within 15 min; full trace + provenance; adjuster gates every payment; every override feeds golden set

Promote if: ≥20/100 route to accelerated, accuracy within ±15% on ≥80%, override rate <15% (after 200 claims, ≥3 adjusters) → expand states, raise cap, add wind/hail

Iterate if: 15-30 route, or accuracy 65-80%, or override 15-25% - threshold or cohort needs tuning

Sunset if: <15 route, accuracy <65%, override >25%, or any bad-faith exposure flagged in legal review

What success looks like after 90 days?
  • 3 slices deployed each moving jointly agreed KPI (cycle time, cost) and stable adoption growth among users
  • each slice proved its ROI (in terms of OPEX savings vs inference cost) and is covered by our analytics
  • slices are developed without claims migration dependency and have observability & tracing from day 1
  • our golden eval set is operational and growing from HITL
  • trust and relationships built over joint decisions made and new opportunities arise
